# 👥 Employee Training Analytics Dashboard \| Power BI

## 📊 Workforce, Training & Employee Performance Insights

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze employee
demographics, training programs, employee attrition, training costs,
performance ratings, and job satisfaction. It provides HR teams and
business leaders with a centralized view of workforce metrics to support
data-driven decisions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🎯 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to transform employee and training data
into actionable HR insights through data preparation, analytical
calculations, and interactive Power BI visualizations.

The dashboard helps users explore workforce composition, monitor
attrition, review training investments, and compare performance and
satisfaction across departments.

## 💼 Business Problem

Organizations invest in employee training and development, but HR teams
need a clear way to monitor workforce size, employee attrition, training
expenses, performance, and satisfaction.

This dashboard helps answer questions such as:

-   How many employees are active or terminated?
-   Which departments have the largest workforces?
-   What is the employee attrition rate?
-   How are training costs distributed?
-   How do performance and satisfaction vary by department?
-   Which recruitment sources show higher employee attrition?

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## 🎯 Dashboard Objectives

1.  Monitor workforce size and employment status.
2.  Track employee attrition across departments and recruitment sources.
3.  Analyze training programs and training costs.
4.  Understand employee demographics and department distribution.
5.  Evaluate performance ratings across the workforce.
6.  Compare satisfaction scores by department.
7.  Provide an interactive executive-level overview of HR metrics.

## 📊 Dashboard Features & Visualizations

### 1. Executive KPI Overview

Summarizes total employees, active employees, terminated employees,
training programs, attrition rate, average training cost, average age,
and average satisfaction score.

### 2. Employee Count by Department

Compares employee headcount across departments such as Production,
IT/IS, Sales, Software Engineering, and Administration.

**Business value:** Helps HR understand workforce distribution and
staffing concentration.

### 3. Attrition by Department

Shows employee attrition across departments.

**Business value:** Helps identify departments that may warrant further
retention analysis.

### 4. Gender Distribution

Visualizes the workforce split by gender.

**Business value:** Supports demographic reporting and workforce
composition analysis.

### 5. Marital Status Distribution

Shows the distribution of employees by marital status.

### 6. Age Group Distribution

Groups employees into age categories to provide an overview of workforce
demographics.

### 7. Training Cost Distribution

Displays how training costs are distributed across cost ranges.

**Business value:** Supports review of training expenditure and budget
planning.

### 8. Performance Distribution

Compares employee counts across performance categories, including Fully
Meets, Exceeds, Needs Improvement, and PIP.

### 9. Employment Status Distribution

Shows the workforce distribution by employment status.

### 10. Attrition by Recruitment Source

Compares attrition across recruitment sources such as Temporary,
Full-Time, and Part-Time.

### 11. Average Satisfaction Score by Department

Compares average satisfaction scores across departments.

### 12. Employee Count by Department and Performance

Shows performance categories across departments for workforce-level
comparisons.

### 13. Top 10 Positions by Employee Count

Ranks job positions by employee count to highlight roles that make up a
large share of the workforce.
